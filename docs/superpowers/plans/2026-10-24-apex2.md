# APEX 2.0 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development or native execution with independent review, milestone by milestone. The owner explicitly requested autonomous implementation, not another approval round.

**Goal:** Deliver the specified real desktop/mobile operating workspace without weakening construction safeguards.

**Architecture:** Retain the modular monolith and existing route contracts. Ordinary tasks and user-authored appointments each have one transactional authority; existing lead follow-ups, gate requirements, crew bookings and inspections remain authoritative projections, never duplicated records. Optional photos reuse evidence/storage/customer-publication boundaries with an explicit non-gate purpose.

**Tech Stack:** Existing React/Vite, Node HTTP API, Zod, PostgreSQL/PGlite, EvidenceStorage, Playwright/Vitest.

**Spec:** `docs/apex2/IMPLEMENTATION.md` records the binding owner directive, source evidence, milestones and known limitations.

## Global constraints

No deployment or merge without approval. No marketing website edits. No fake production business data. No invented logo. Photos are optional documentation, never lifecycle gates. Preserve approval, inspection, procurement, closure, idempotency and authorization safeguards. Server-authoritative state shared between desktop/mobile. One canonical ordinary checklist and unified scheduling projection. Preserve existing deep links.

## Review focus

- Scope changes and sign-out must not show prior user's private records or upload bytes.
- Lost acknowledgement/retry must not duplicate tasks, appointments, recurrence or photos.
- Job closure must serialize against task/event/photo writes.
- Civil dates and month boundaries must not shift due to timezone conversion.
- Unknown division rules must not become invented approvals or pool gates silently imposed on another division.
