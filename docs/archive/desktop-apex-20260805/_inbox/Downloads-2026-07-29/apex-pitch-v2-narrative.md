## Slide 6 — The ask *(dark)*

**Kicker:** THE ASK

**Headline:** Approve the direction. Then three answers.

**The approval, set large in amber:** *The four stages, in that order.*
Sub-line: Nothing below matters if the shape is wrong. If it's right, these three unlock the rest.

**Three numbered items:**

1. **Your cost-plus agreement** — so we can see what it defines as reimbursable cost. Every dollar of
   that $13,750 waits on this one document.
2. **Ten minutes with your bookkeeper** — is QuickBooks using Projects? Classes? One company file or
   three? Three factual questions. They unlock cost capture, and cost capture is what commission runs on.
3. **How allowances settle** — the turf allowance is $5,000 and the turf comes in at $6,200. Does the
   fee recalculate on the real number, or stay fixed at estimate? That answer defines what "profit"
   means, and profit is what commission pays on.

**Two that cost nothing, smaller:**

- Cap builds at three at a time — same output, five weeks faster for every customer.
- Log crew days on the next two jobs — one number per phase.
