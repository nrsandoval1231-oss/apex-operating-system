## Evidence and decision

The current `GET /api/today` comes exclusively from `readCardSnapshot()` of Jobs and `ActionCardSchema` requires `jobId`; the frozen Brief consumes those same cards. ## Slice 1 packet: one actionable pre-contract Today lane

Objective: staff can see and resolve a due or missing next action for every open pre-contract opportunity from Today, using durable PostgreSQL records. Keep existing construction Today cards and frozen daily Brief behavior intact. No procurement or new Job identity in this slice.

Allowed source surfaces: one forward-only `packages/database/migrations/0034_*` migration; narrow lead-follow-up service and tests in `packages/gate-service/src/`; a small lead-follow-up contract in `packages/contracts/src/`; staff-only routes and tests in `apps/gate-api/src/server.ts` / `server.test.ts`; `apps/apex-os/src/pages/TodayFeed.tsx`, a new opportunity detail page and route in `App.tsx`, narrow client hook/form and focused browser test; `docs/decisions/` for one explicit authority amendment; `STATUS.md` and `NEXT.md` after verification. Extend exact files only when required by imports or tests, with reason in completion artifact. Exclude Designer quantity/pricing logic, signed Proposal/Job transitions, current Job card contract and derivation, `daily_briefs` stored shape, procurement, QuickBooks, Monday/HubSpot adapters, website, cinematic assets, and real integrations.

Persistence: attach follow-ups to `lead_id`, with a required next action, civil due date, accountable staff user, creator, completion actor/time, and timestamps. Retain completed history. Enforce at most one open follow-up per Lead in PostgreSQL; use an atomic create/replace-or-complete operation with deterministic conflict behavior and idempotent retry. Do not modify `leads.status` when a follow-up is completed. A signed Lead/Job is excluded from pre-contract Today and cannot acquire another pre-contract follow-up. A rejected Lead is excluded. Database FK/check/index and service transaction must enforce the invariant; no blind upsert that loses a completed action. Admin/office may manage; field/customer cannot read or mutate pre-contract data.

Projection: a separate, typed pre-contract Today result keyed by `leadId`, never a fabricated `jobId`. Include actionable rows for (a) an open follow-up due today or overdue, and (b) an unsigned/unrejected Lead with no open follow-up, to prompt a next action. An issued Proposal without a follow-up should identify the Proposal state truthfully; do not infer that a customer has been contacted. Future-due follow-ups may be visible in the opportunity detail, but must not be labeled overdue. Deterministic ordering: overdue first by due date, then due today, then missing next action; stable Lead ID tie-break. All row links land on the staff opportunity record, where an authorized user can schedule or complete the action; completion reloads Today from persisted state. Today's construction count/empty copy must account for both lanes so it cannot say “all clear” while pre-contract actions exist. Show a scoped error when one lane fails rather than claim complete coverage.

Brief: preserve `GET /api/brief` and `DailyBriefSchema` frozen snapshots. Name pre-contract follow-up as a coverage gap in Brief until a later separately validated integration; do not silently append these rows to Job cards or rewrite delivered briefs.
