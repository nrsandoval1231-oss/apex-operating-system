## P-001, smallest procurement vertical after F-001

Purpose: on a signed, mutable Job, staff can select a material requirement from its **current approved Designer takeoff**, record a real-world vendor/order reference and expected delivery, enter cumulative received quantity, and see the outstanding amount and a dated blocker. This is human-entered operational coordination, not issuing a purchase order or posting accounting commitments.

Authority: canonical `job_id` and `current_takeoff_revision_id` from PostgreSQL; validate the approved revision's existing quantity envelope and digest. Pin each requirement to that revision ID and its quantity code, value, and unit. Only permit material-relevant codes explicitly selected by a staff user from the approved quantity vocabulary; never transform geometry, price lines, or workbook display rows into an inferred bill of materials. Preserve the approved quantity and unit unchanged as the reference. An order quantity and vendor are explicitly entered, with the same unit (no implicit conversion); order reference can point to the external document. A revised approved takeoff does not silently rewrite prior commitments: flag them as tied to an earlier revision and require a human to reconcile before current-revision blocker claims. No procurement row is created before Job acceptance.

Small durable model: one tracked material line per `(job_id, revision_id, quantity_code)` for the pilot, with required quantity snapshot, manually entered order quantity, vendor, order/reference, user-entered needed-by and expected-delivery civil dates, cumulative received quantity, actor/timestamps, and optional note. Database checks/FKs and a Job row lock enforce same-Job revision, nonnegative finite quantities, same-unit semantics, `received <= ordered` where ordered, and closed/cancelled Job refusal. Idempotent mutation and row locking must prevent duplicate lines or lost concurrent receipts. Do not claim support for split orders, vendor catalog, automated purchasing, subcontractor payment, cost commitments, or generalized inventory; those need separate authority and model.

Owner experience: a compact Procurement section on Project Workspace shows source revision/code/unit, needed, ordered, received, outstanding, vendor/reference, expected date, and who last changed it. Admin/office can create/update the line and log receipt; field/customer cannot see internal vendor/order details or mutate them. Add a Today blocker only for a tracked line with a **recorded** needed-by date reached while required receipt remains short, or an explicitly ordered line whose expected date has passed with undelivered quantity. Link to that Project section and resolve automatically when receipts/date are corrected. A Job with no tracked lines is “procurement not tracked,” not “materials clear.” Avoid a separate procurement status state machine: derive `unplanned`, `ordered`, `partial`, `received`, and overdue presentation from facts. Do not block construction Gate release from procurement data without a separate approved Gate requirement.
